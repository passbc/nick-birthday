# Birthday Website ❤️

## Add your personal content
1. Open `index.html`.
2. Find:
   `const NAME="My Special One";`
   and replace it with his name.
3. Put your files inside the `media` folder:
   - `photo1.jpg`
   - `photo2.jpg`
   - `photo3.jpg`
   - `photo4.jpg`
   - `photo5.jpg`
   - `video1.mp4`
   - `birthday-music.mp3`
   - `trainer-photo.jpg`
4. Edit the birthday letter and the "Why he is special" section directly in `index.html`.

## Important about music
Browsers normally block automatic audio until the visitor interacts with the page. This website starts the music when he clicks the surprise/inbox, and there is also a music button.

## Make it a live link
The folder is ready to upload to a static hosting service such as GitHub Pages or Netlify. Upload the whole folder (including `media`) and share the generated website link.

No backend/database is required.
